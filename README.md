# **Amazon Sales Analysis Using SQL Queries**
## **Project Overview**
This project involves analyzing sales data for Amazon using SQL queries to extract insights about product pricing, discounts, reviews, and customer preferences. The queries target various aspects of product performance, such as filtering by price, discount, and review content, enabling data-driven decisions for pricing strategies and marketing efforts.

## **SQL Queries**
The following SQL queries were used to extract valuable insights from the dataset:

### **List all products with a discounted price below ₹500.**
- Helps identify affordable products for budget-conscious customers.
### **Find products with a discount percentage of 50% or more.**
- Allows identification of high-discount products, potentially used for clearance sales or promotions.
### **Retrieve all products where the name contains the word "Cable."**
- Uncovers demand for specific products like cables, useful for inventory and marketing decisions.
### **Display the difference between the average of the actual price and the discounted price for each product.**
- Measures the price-value perception, highlighting products perceived as offering better value.
### **Query reviews that mention "fast charging" in their content.**
- Highlights customer preferences for fast charging, guiding product development and marketing.
### **Identify products with a discount percentage between 20% and 40%.**
- Targets mid-range discounts, ideal for customers looking for balance between affordability and quality.
### **Find products that have an actual price above ₹1,000 and are rated 4 stars or above.**
- Identifies premium products that attract discerning buyers willing to invest in quality.
### **Find products where the discounted price ends with a 9.**
- Implements psychological pricing strategies, making products appear more affordable.
### **Display review contents that contain words like worst, waste, poor, or not good.**
- Helps identify areas for improvement in products and customer satisfaction.
### **List all products where the category includes "Accessories."**
- Focuses on the demand for accessories, enabling targeted marketing strategies.
## **Key Insights**
The analysis provided the following insights:

### **Affordable Products with High Discounts**
- Many products are discounted below ₹500, attracting price-sensitive customers. This can be leveraged in targeted marketing campaigns.
### **High Discount Opportunities**
- A large portion of the catalog offers discounts of 50% or more, which might indicate clearance sales or high initial pricing. Optimizing these discounts could boost sales.
### **Demand for Specific Products**
- "Cable" products are highly demanded, suggesting potential inventory expansion and marketing campaigns around cables.
### **Price-Value Perception**
- Products with significant price differences between the actual and discounted price provide value to customers, making them attractive for promotions.
### **Customer Preferences in Reviews**
- Reviews mentioning "fast charging" indicate a strong customer preference for fast charging capabilities. Highlighting this feature can increase sales.
### **Mid-Range Discount Products**
- Products with discounts between 20% and 40% strike a balance between affordability and profitability, ideal for mid-range buyers.
### **Premium Products with High Ratings**
- High-priced products with 4+ stars ratings appeal to quality-conscious customers, ideal for premium marketing channels.
### **Psychological Pricing Strategies**
- Products with discounted prices ending in '9' use psychological pricing, potentially making the prices seem lower. This strategy can be further optimized.
### **Negative Customer Feedback**
- Reviews with terms like "worst," "waste," or "poor" reveal areas for product or service improvement, helping to reduce negative reviews.
### **Accessory Demand**
- There is significant demand for products categorized as "Accessories." Understanding the types of accessories customers prefer can help refine the product lineup.
## **Conclusion**
The **Amazon Sales Analysis** using SQL queries has provided insightful data on product performance, customer preferences, and pricing strategies. Key findings include effective use of high discounts, understanding demand for specific products, and identifying features like fast charging that customers value.

By focusing on the insights, such as optimizing pricing, improving product features, and addressing negative reviews, Amazon can drive higher sales and customer satisfaction. Additionally, understanding customer behavior through review content and sales trends will help in strategic decision-making for inventory management, product development, and marketing.
